# References

The public references and the comparisons made against them are kept in two files:

- [references/sources.md](references/sources.md): the public recordings used, what each shows and what it was used for.
- [references/tuning-log.md](references/tuning-log.md): every comparison of a render against them, in order, with what was measured and what changed.

The measurement tools used for the comparisons are in [`tools/measure/`](../tools/measure/).
